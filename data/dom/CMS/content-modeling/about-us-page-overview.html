About Us Page

Defines the structure, fields, and schema to design the About Us page of your website.

Overview
Schema

Description

Content Models let you structure and organize your content. A content model can have single or multiple content types with fields that serve as templates for your content.

The About Us page is a section of a website that provides information about the company or organization that owns the website.

The About Us Page Content Model contains four Content Types named About Us Page, Contact, Hero Banner, and Our Team, and three Global Fields named SEO, Section, and Section with Buckets that includes the required fields to help you build a suitable About Us page for your website.

Who can import

  • Stack administrators
